Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchase? Open Boothie → Settings tab → Restore Purchases.
How do I cancel my subscription? iOS Settings → tap your Apple ID → Subscriptions → Boothie → Cancel Subscription.
Will my strips sync across devices? Not yet — strips are saved locally on your device. iCloud sync is coming soon.
Can I use Boothie without an account? Yes! Boothie works without sign-in. No account needed.
Does Boothie collect my photos? No. All photos stay on your device. We never upload or access them.
Why is the 3-day trial not starting? Trial requires an eligible Apple ID. If you've used a trial before, you may not be eligible again. Contact us if you have issues.
