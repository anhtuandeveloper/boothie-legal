Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new device?

Open Boothie and tap the gold Pro button at the top-right of the Home screen (or open the Profile tab and tap the Boothie Premium banner). In the paywall, tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID that you used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions. Find Boothie in the list and tap Cancel Subscription. You will keep Premium access until the end of the current billing period.

I was charged but Premium is not active.

First, tap the gold Pro button at the top-right of the Home screen to open the paywall, then tap Restore Purchases. If that does not work, email us with your Apple receipt (Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Boothie) and we will sort it out.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Apple declines and you believe you have a genuine issue, email us and we will advocate for you.

Does Boothie upload my photos?

No. Every photo and strip stays on your device. Boothie does not have a server that stores or syncs your content. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Will my strips sync across my iPhone and iPad?

Not yet. Cross-device sync is on our roadmap. For now you can save strips to your Photos library, which can sync through iCloud Photos.

Why did the 3-day free trial not start for me?

Apple only allows one free trial per Apple ID per subscription group. If you have used the trial before (even in another app that shares our subscription group), Apple will not grant it again. This is an Apple policy, not a Boothie limitation.

Can I use Boothie without signing up?

Yes. Boothie does not require an account. You can open the app and start capturing right away.

Report a bug

Email tuan.iosdev@gmail.com and include:

  • iPhone model (for example, iPhone 15 Pro)
  • iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version)
  • Boothie version (shown in the Profile tab)
  • Steps that caused the issue
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible
