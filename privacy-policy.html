Overview

Your privacy matters to us

Boothie is a photobooth strip app for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our app, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it as simple as possible.

Data Collection

What we collect

Camera & Photos

Boothie requires camera access to capture photos for your photobooth strips. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers — they stay entirely on your device unless you choose to share them. If you grant photo library access, we only write the finished strip to your library; we never read or analyze existing photos.

Purchase Information

All in-app purchases are handled by Apple. We receive only a confirmation of your purchase status — we never see your payment details, credit card number, or Apple ID credentials.

Usage & Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used in aggregate. This may include:

  • Which templates and features are most popular
  • Session length and frequency
  • Crash reports and error logs
  • Device model, OS version, and app version

None of this data is linked to your personal identity.

Advertising

The free version of Boothie shows ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to show relevant ads. You can opt out in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Security

How we protect your data

Photos you take with Boothie are stored only on your device — we don't operate servers that hold your photos. Analytics data is processed by Firebase on Google's infrastructure under industry-standard security practices.

We retain analytics data for up to 14 months per Firebase's default settings. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us.

Children

Children's privacy

Boothie is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have done so inadvertently, please contact us and we will delete that information promptly.

Your Rights

Control over your data

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold. You can also:

  • Opt out of analytics tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Limit ad personalization in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising
  • Contact us to request deletion of any analytics data tied to your device
Updates

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
